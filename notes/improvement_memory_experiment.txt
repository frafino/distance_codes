- BP: estimates which errors are more likely.
- OSD (Ordered Statistics Decoding): uses those estimates to find a correction that satisfies the syndrome exactly.
- LSD (Localized Statistics Decoding): identify relatively small regions associated with errors and solve local systems.
- Relay-BP: Improve the iterative message-passing procedure so that it can find good corrections despite cycles and symmetries.
- Tesseract: searches for combinations of error mechanisms whose combined detector effect matches the observed syndrome.
Its objective is to identify a low-cost, highly probable error configuration. Graph search with techniques including A*, Dijkstra-style search, beam-search heuristics, and pruning.
